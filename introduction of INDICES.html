📖 KEY WORDS

Important Words to Know (Terminology)

Index (Indices)
The small number written at the top right. It tells you how many times to multiply the base by itself. "Indices" is just the plural of "index".
Base
The big number at the bottom. It is the number being multiplied by itself.
Power / Exponent
Another name for index. All three words — index, power, and exponent — mean the same small number on top!
Index Notation
Writing a number using a base and an index. Example: 2³ instead of writing 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 out in full.
Laws of Indices
Special shortcut rules that help us work with index notation quickly, without multiplying everything out!
Evaluate / Simplify
Evaluate = find the number value. Simplify = write in a shorter, neater index form.

🌍 WHY IT MATTERS

Where Do We Use Indices in Real Life?

Indices are not just maths tricks — they are used everywhere in the real world! Here are some cool examples:

💾
Computer Storage
1 Kilobyte = 2¹⁰ bytes. 1 Gigabyte = 2³⁰ bytes!
2¹⁰ = 1,024
📐
Area of a Square
Area = side × side = side². A 5m room has area = 5² = 25 m²
5² = 25 m²
📦
Volume of a Box
Volume = side³. A box of 3 cm sides = 3³ = 27 cm³
3³ = 27 cm³
🦠
Bacteria Growth
Bacteria doubles every hour. After 4 hours: 2⁴ = 16 bacteria
2⁴ = 16
🌍
Very Big Numbers
The mass of Earth = 6 × 10²⁴ kg — easier to write using indices!
10²⁴
💰
Bank Savings
Money growing in a bank uses powers — more time = more growth!
A × rⁿ
