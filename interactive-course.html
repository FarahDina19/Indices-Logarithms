📖 Understanding Indices & Logarithms

What is a Logarithm?

A logarithm is the inverse of exponentiation:

If ax = b, then loga(b) = x

Question: "To what power must we raise a to get b?"

Example: Exponential vs Logarithmic

Exponential Form:

2³ = 8 (2 raised to power 3 equals 8)

Logarithmic Form:

log₂(8) = 3 (logarithm base 2 of 8 equals 3)

Same relationship, different notation!
